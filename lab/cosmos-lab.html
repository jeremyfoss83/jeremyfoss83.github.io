02 / Mathematical lens

When motion becomes geometry.

Resonance mode preserves orbital trails and emphasizes bodies whose periods approach simple ratios. Repeated motion accumulates into rosettes, braids, and near-infinite loops—making an abstract relationship visible.

03 / Under the instrument

A visual model with explicit assumptions.

This is a front-end systems and data-visualization experiment informed by orbital mechanics—not aerospace engineering analysis or a mission-design tool.

01

Force model

Every body accelerates every other body through a softened Newtonian inverse-square relationship.

02

Numerical model

A fixed-step velocity-Verlet integrator advances position and velocity independently of display refresh. Scaled units and softening favor stable, legible interaction over scientific precision.

03

Resonance

Period ratios and persistent paths reveal repeating phase relationships. The geometry is an interpretive layer over the same simulation state.

04

Procedural depth

Seeded star tiles are generated around the camera as it moves, then recycled outside the view. The field feels unbounded without storing an infinite scene.

05

Limits

The model is planar; radii and vectors are visually exaggerated; collisions, relativity, tides, and three-dimensional inclination are omitted. Telemetry is diagnostic—not a claim of physical validation.

06

Performance & access

Canvas rendering is resolution-aware, controls remain available in semantic HTML, live state is announced, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.
