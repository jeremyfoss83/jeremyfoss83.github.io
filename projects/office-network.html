Network Design · Case Study 04

Enterprise Three-Floor Office Network

A scalable physical and logical design combining hierarchical architecture, address planning, network segmentation, wireless coverage, security concepts, and implementation costs.

ArtifactCTS 1305 final project
TermSummer 2024
ToolsMicrosoft Visio
Outcome300 / 300

The design brief

The project called for an office network capable of supporting more than 100 concurrent users across three floors while remaining understandable, secure, and expandable.

Architecture and addressing

I used a three-tier core, distribution, and access architecture. The addressing plan began with 192.168.0.0/16 and assigned /22 floor subnets, alongside VLANs 10, 20, and 30 plus management and guest network considerations.

  • Planned DHCP ranges and reserved static addresses.
  • Documented physical and logical topologies in Visio.
  • Specified Cisco and Ubiquiti equipment.
  • Selected fiber and Cat6A cabling by network role.
  • Planned wireless access-point coverage.
  • Prepared an implementation cost estimate.

Security and scalability

The design incorporated WPA3-Enterprise and 802.1X/RADIUS concepts, guest isolation, firewalls, IDS/IPS, SIEM, vulnerability scanning, and endpoint-security considerations. Future-proofing documentation explained how the structure could grow.

What I would improve next

I would rebuild the topology in a network simulator, validate routing and segmentation rules, add a formal risk register, and compare the budget against a second vendor configuration.
