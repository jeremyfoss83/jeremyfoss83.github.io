Life OS

An ongoing, AI-assisted desktop prototype built around a simple rule: an interpretation should stay connected to the evidence behind it.

ContextIndependent prototype
Development2026
StackTypeScript · Electron
My roleDirection & requirements

The problem

A personal information tool can become confusing when stored facts, reflections, and AI-generated conclusions are presented as if they were the same thing. I wanted a system where the user could see those distinctions and retain control of their records.

My contribution

I defined the product direction, evidence rules, privacy requirements, and staged acceptance criteria, and worked with AI coding agents on implementation and validation.

  • Separate recorded facts, user reflections, and AI interpretations.
  • Connect claims to supporting records.
  • Allow corrections, rejection, export, and deletion.
  • Keep a local-first architecture with encrypted storage and recovery flows.

What the prototype includes

Development work includes permissions, record history, export and deletion controls, a Trust Center, and automated tests. The application uses Node.js, TypeScript, and Electron.

Project status: This is an evolving private prototype. Implementation was AI-assisted; it is not presented as independently hand-coded software or a finished production service.

What I learned

Building with AI still requires clear decisions about behavior, scope, and evidence. Breaking the work into reviewable stages made it easier to spot gaps between a feature being implemented and a feature being ready to trust.

What I would improve next

Complete end-to-end user acceptance checks, test recovery and export paths on the target desktop environment, and make the evidence review flow easier to use.
